## Market Cipher B - Core Indicators

### 1. Blue Momentum Waves
The primary indicator for identifying trends and reversals.

| Signal | Description | Trading Implication |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Light Blue → Dark Blue** | Trend change occurring | Potential entry/exit point |
| **Above Zero Line** | Bullish momentum | Look for long entries |
| **Below Zero Line** | Bearish momentum | Look for short entries |
| **Higher Low Pattern** | Bullish divergence forming | Long entry setup |
| **Lower High Pattern** | Bearish divergence forming | Short entry setup |

**Anchor Wave**: The first, deep wave that establishes the initial momentum direction. Should be "fat and deep" for strong signals.

**Trigger Wave**: The smaller wave following the anchor wave. Entry is taken when the trigger wave confirms direction.

### 2. Money Flow Wave
Shows institutional money movement in and out of an asset.

| Color | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Green** | Money flowing IN | Bullish - look for longs |
| **Red** | Money flowing OUT | Bearish - look for shorts |
| **Smooth & Rounded** | Predictable movement | Higher probability trades |
| **Choppy/Jagged** | Unpredictable | AVOID trading |

**Key Rule**: "Enter in the red, exit in the green" - Buy when money flow is red (oversold), sell when green (overbought).

### 3. Green Dot (Buy Signal)
**THE most important bullish signal in Market Cipher**

| Condition | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Appearance** | Green dot appears on oversold cross-up |
| **Location** | Below the blue momentum waves |
| **Confirmation** | Blue waves making higher low with gap |
| **Best Setup** | Deep anchor wave + trigger wave + green dot |

**Entry Criteria for Green Dot Long**:
1. Price must be ABOVE the 200 EMA (from DBSI)
2. Blue waves show higher low with GAP between waves
3. Price also makes higher low
4. Green dot appears on cross-up of second wave

### 4. Red Dot (Sell Signal)
The bearish counterpart to the green dot.

| Condition | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Appearance** | Red dot appears on overbought cross-down |
| **Location** | Above the blue momentum waves |
| **Confirmation** | Blue waves making lower high with gap |

**Entry Criteria for Red Dot Short**:
1. Price must be BELOW the 200 EMA
2. Blue waves show lower high with GAP between waves
3. Price also makes lower high
4. Red dot appears on cross-down of second wave

### 5. VWAP (Yellow Waves)
Volume Weighted Average Price oscillator.

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Below Blue Waves** | Bullish - supports long entries |
| **Above Blue Waves** | Bearish - supports short entries |
| **Inward Curvature** | Potential reversal incoming |
| **Cross Above Zero** | Long signal |
| **Cross Below Zero** | Short signal |

### 6. RSI Indicators
Smoothed RSI and Stochastic RSI combined.

| Condition | Interpretation |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Green & Tight** | Bullish - building strength |
| **Red & Spreading** | Bearish - losing momentum |
| **Low Levels** | Oversold - potential bottom |
| **High Levels** | Overbought - potential top |

## Market Cipher A - Warning Signals

### Yellow X (Manipulation/Exit Warning)
**CRITICAL SIGNAL** - Indicates whale manipulation or strong exit signal.

| Context | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **At Resistance** | Whales selling/distributing |
| **After Rally** | Take profit warning |
| **Multiple Yellow X's** | Strong bearish pressure |

### Red X (Hard Warning)
Strong bearish signal indicating downward pressure.

| Usage |
|-------|
| Exit long positions |
| Consider short entries |
| Increase caution |

### Blood Diamond (Harshest Warning)
**THE most bearish signal** - Indicates potential major reversal.

| Appearance | Action |
|------------|--------|
| Red diamond shape | Immediately consider exiting longs |
| After extended rally | High probability reversal |
| With other bearish signals | Very strong short setup |

### Small Red Diamond (First Warning)
Early warning signal before larger moves.

| Context | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| First appearance | Initial caution signal |
| Before Blood Diamond | Warning escalation |

### Yellow Diamond (Continuation)
Indicates trend continuation rather than reversal.

### Blue Triangle (Reversal)
Signals potential trend reversal point.

### Green Dot (Cipher A)
Additional bullish confirmation when aligned with Cipher B green dot.

### Moving Average Ribbons
| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **White to Blue (Full Color)** | Bullish trend |
| **Grayed Out** | Bearish trend |

## Market Cipher DBSI (Dual Band Strength Index)

### 200-Period Dynamic EMA
| Color | Position | Meaning |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **Green** | Price above | Bullish trend - ONLY LONGS |
| **Red** | Price below | Bearish trend - ONLY SHORTS |

### DBSI Numbers (-5 to +31)
Combines 36 separate momentum indicators into a single score.

| Score | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| **14+** | Strong signal |
| **-2 to -5** | Weak opposing pressure |
| **27+** | Extremely strong |

**Entry Signal**: Large number (14+) on one side vs low negative (-2 to -5) on opposite side.

**Exit Signal**: Strong signal weakening (e.g., 27 → 8) while opposite side increases.

## Complete Entry Checklist

### Long Entry Setup
- [ ] Price ABOVE 200 EMA (DBSI)
- [ ] Blue waves showing higher low with GAP
- [ ] Price making higher low
- [ ] Green dot appearing
- [ ] Money flow smooth and curving up (or red for "buy the dip")
- [ ] RSI green and tight, building from low
- [ ] VWAP below blue waves
- [ ] Higher timeframe confirms bullish bias

### Short Entry Setup
- [ ] Price BELOW 200 EMA (DBSI)
- [ ] Blue waves showing lower high with GAP
- [ ] Price making lower high
- [ ] Red dot appearing
- [ ] Money flow red and curving down
- [ ] RSI red and spreading
- [ ] VWAP above blue waves
- [ ] Higher timeframe confirms bearish bias

## The Blue Wave Strategy (Core Strategy)

1. **Wait for Anchor Wave**: Deep, fat initial wave
2. **Confirm Trigger Wave**: Smaller follow-up wave
3. **Check Money Flow**: Must be smooth, not choppy
4. **Entry**: When trigger wave color changes (light→dark blue)
5. **Exit**: When money flow reverses or blood diamond appears

**Key Principle**: Patience - only trade when setup is clear with smooth money flow.

## Divergence Trading

### Bullish Divergence
```
Price: Makes LOWER LOW
Blue Waves: Make HIGHER LOW
Signal: Potential bullish reversal
```

### Bearish Divergence
```
Price: Makes HIGHER HIGH
Blue Waves: Make LOWER HIGH
Signal: Potential bearish reversal
```

**Important**: The second "hump" of the divergence must be FULLY FORMED before entering. Wait for confirmation!

**Stronger Divergences**: When the momentum waves cross the upper or lower blue lines during the divergence pattern.

## Multi-Timeframe Analysis

**Critical Rule**: Higher timeframes MUST back up lower timeframe entries.

| Timeframe | Use |
|-----------|-----|
| **Daily/Weekly** | Determine overall trend direction |
| **4H/12H** | Confirm bias and major levels |
| **1H/30M** | Fine-tune entries |
| **15M/5M** | Scalp entries (only with HTF confirmation) |

**Example**: A green dot on 4-minute chart is ONLY valid if the hourly chart also shows bullish money flow, RSI, and momentum.
