# Market Cipher Signals

This document provides a comprehensive guide to all signals generated by Market Cipher and how to interpret them.

## Primary Signals

### Blue Dot (Strong Buy Signal)

The blue dot is Market Cipher's strongest bullish signal. It appears when multiple bullish conditions align simultaneously.

**Requirements for Blue Dot:**
- RSI in oversold territory (typically below 30)
- Bullish divergence on momentum waves
- Money flow showing reversal from red to green
- Price at or near support level

**How to Trade:**
```
Entry: At or slightly above the blue dot candle close
Stop Loss: Below the recent swing low (typically 1-2% below entry)
Take Profit: 2:1 minimum risk/reward ratio
```

**Strength Indicators:**
- Blue dot at major support = Strongest
- Blue dot with high volume = Very strong
- Blue dot on higher timeframe = More reliable
- Multiple blue dots in sequence = Accumulation zone

### Red X (Strong Sell Signal)

The red X is Market Cipher's strongest bearish signal. It appears when multiple bearish conditions align.

**Requirements for Red X:**
- RSI in overbought territory (typically above 70)
- Bearish divergence on momentum waves
- Money flow showing reversal from green to red
- Price at or near resistance level

**How to Trade:**
```
Entry: At or slightly below the red X candle close
Stop Loss: Above the recent swing high (typically 1-2% above entry)
Take Profit: 2:1 minimum risk/reward ratio
```

## Momentum Wave Signals

### Wave Colors

| Color | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Green** | Bullish momentum | Look for longs |
| **Red** | Bearish momentum | Look for shorts |
| **Light Green** | Weakening bullish | Prepare for reversal |
| **Light Red** | Weakening bearish | Prepare for reversal |

### Wave Crosses

**Bullish Cross:**
- Waves cross from below zero to above
- Indicates momentum shift to bullish
- Best when combined with other confluence

**Bearish Cross:**
- Waves cross from above zero to below
- Indicates momentum shift to bearish
- Best when combined with other confluence

### Divergences

**Bullish Divergence:**
```
Price: Makes lower low
Waves: Make higher low
Signal: Potential bullish reversal
```

**Bearish Divergence:**
```
Price: Makes higher high
Waves: Make lower high
Signal: Potential bearish reversal
```

**Hidden Bullish Divergence:**
```
Price: Makes higher low
Waves: Make lower low
Signal: Trend continuation (bullish)
```

**Hidden Bearish Divergence:**
```
Price: Makes lower high
Waves: Make higher high
Signal: Trend continuation (bearish)
```

## Money Flow Signals

### Money Flow Colors

| Color | Meaning | Interpretation |
|-------|---------|----------------|
| **Bright Green** | Strong buying pressure | Bulls in control |
| **Dark Green** | Moderate buying | Bullish bias |
| **Bright Red** | Strong selling pressure | Bears in control |
| **Dark Red** | Moderate selling | Bearish bias |
| **Gray** | Neutral/consolidation | Wait for direction |

### Money Flow Transitions

**Green to Red Transition:**
- Indicates shift from buying to selling
- Potential short opportunity
- Most powerful at resistance levels

**Red to Green Transition:**
- Indicates shift from selling to buying
- Potential long opportunity
- Most powerful at support levels

## DBSI Signals

The Dollar Buy/Sell Index measures institutional activity.

### Large Green Bars
- Significant institutional buying
- Often precedes upward moves
- Confirmation of bullish bias

### Large Red Bars
- Significant institutional selling
- Often precedes downward moves
- Confirmation of bearish bias

### Small/No Bars
- Low institutional activity
- Market in consolidation
- Wait for larger bars before trading

## Signal Confluence Matrix

Use this matrix to assess signal strength:

| Confluence Level | Signals Required | Confidence |
|-----------------|------------------|------------|
| **Weak** | 1-2 signals | 40-50% |
| **Moderate** | 3-4 signals | 60-70% |
| **Strong** | 5-6 signals | 75-85% |
| **Very Strong** | 7+ signals | 85-95% |

### Confluence Checklist

**For Long Trades:**
- [ ] Blue dot present
- [ ] Money flow green
- [ ] RSI oversold or rising
- [ ] Bullish divergence
- [ ] Price at support
- [ ] Higher timeframe bullish
- [ ] Volume confirmation
- [ ] DBSI showing buying

**For Short Trades:**
- [ ] Red X present
- [ ] Money flow red
- [ ] RSI overbought or falling
- [ ] Bearish divergence
- [ ] Price at resistance
- [ ] Higher timeframe bearish
- [ ] Volume confirmation
- [ ] DBSI showing selling

## Signal Timing

### When to Enter

1. **Aggressive Entry:** Immediately on signal appearance
2. **Conservative Entry:** On candle close after signal
3. **Confirmation Entry:** After next candle confirms direction

### When to Avoid Signals

- During major news events
- In choppy/ranging markets without clear trend
- When signal contradicts higher timeframe trend
- When volume is unusually low
- During market open/close volatility
