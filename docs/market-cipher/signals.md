## Market Cipher B - Core Indicators

### 1. Blue Momentum Waves

The primary indicator for identifying trends and reversals.

| Signal | Description | Trading Implication |
|--------|-------------|---------------------|
| **Light Blue → Dark Blue** | Trend change occurring | Potential entry/exit point |
| **Above Zero Line** | Bullish momentum | Look for long entries |
| **Below Zero Line** | Bearish momentum | Look for short entries |
| **Higher Low Pattern** | Bullish divergence forming | Long entry setup |
| **Lower High Pattern** | Bearish divergence forming | Short entry setup |

**Anchor Wave**: The first, deep wave that establishes the initial momentum direction. Should be "fat and deep" for strong signals.

**Trigger Wave**: The smaller wave following the anchor wave. Entry is taken when the trigger wave confirms direction.

### 2. Money Flow Wave

Shows institutional money movement in and out of an asset.

| Color | Meaning | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Green** | Money flowing IN | Bullish - look for longs |
| **Red** | Money flowing OUT | Bearish - look for shorts |
| **Smooth & Rounded** | Predictable movement | Higher probability trades |
| **Choppy/Jagged** | Unpredictable | AVOID trading |

**Key Rule**: "Enter in the red, exit in the green" - Buy when money flow is red (oversold), sell when green (overbought).

**Money Flow as Lifeblood**: The money flow indicator represents the "lifeblood" of an asset. Increasing money flow leads to price appreciation; decreasing money flow leads to price depreciation.

### 3. Green Dot (Buy Signal)

**THE most important bullish signal in Market Cipher**

| Condition | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Appearance** | Green dot appears on oversold cross-up |
| **Location** | Below the blue momentum waves |
| **Confirmation** | Blue waves making higher low with gap |
| **Best Setup** | Deep anchor wave + trigger wave + green dot |

**Entry Criteria for Green Dot Long**:
1. Price must be ABOVE the 200 EMA (from DBSI)
2. Blue waves show higher low with GAP between waves
3. Price also makes higher low
4. Green dot appears on cross-up of second wave

### 4. VWAP (Yellow Waves)

Volume Weighted Average Price oscillator.

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Below Blue Waves** | Bullish - supports long entries |
| **Above Blue Waves** | Bearish - supports short entries |
| **Inward Curvature** | Potential reversal incoming |
| **Cross Above Zero** | Long signal |
| **Cross Below Zero** | Short signal |

### 5. RSI Indicators

| Condition | Interpretation |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Green & Tight** | Bullish - building strength |
| **Red & Spreading** | Bearish - losing momentum |
| **Low Levels** | Oversold - weak, needs time to build |
| **High Levels** | Overbought - potential top |

## Market Cipher A - Warning Signals

### Yellow X (Manipulation/Exit Warning)

**CRITICAL SIGNAL** - Indicates whale manipulation or strong exit signal.

| Context | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **At Resistance** | Whales selling/distributing |
| **After Rally** | Take profit warning |
| **Multiple Yellow X's** | Strong bearish pressure |
| **Near Liquidation Zones** | Manipulation to trigger stops |

### Blood Diamond (Harshest Warning)

**THE most bearish signal** - Indicates potential major reversal.

| Appearance | Action |
|------------|--------|
| Red diamond shape | Immediately exit longs |
| After extended rally | High probability reversal |
| With other bearish signals | Very strong short setup |

### Red X (Hard Warning)

Strong bearish signal indicating downward pressure. Exit longs, consider shorts.

### Small Red Diamond (First Warning)

Early warning signal before larger moves.

### Yellow Diamond (Continuation)

Indicates trend continuation rather than reversal.

### Blue Triangle (Reversal)

Signals potential trend reversal point.

### Moving Average Ribbons

| Color | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **White to Blue (Full Color)** | Bullish trend |
| **Grayed Out** | Bearish trend |

## Market Cipher DBSI

### 200-Period Dynamic EMA

| Color | Position | Meaning |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **Green** | Price above | Bullish trend - ONLY LONGS |
| **Red** | Price below | Bearish trend - ONLY SHORTS |

### DBSI Numbers (-5 to +31)

Combines 36 separate momentum indicators into a single score.

| Score | Interpretation |
|-------|----------------|
| **14+** | Strong signal |
| **-2 to -5** | Weak opposing pressure |
| **27+** | Extremely strong |

**Entry Signal**: Large number (14+) on one side vs low negative (-2 to -5) on opposite side.

**Exit Signal**: Strong signal weakening (e.g., 27 → 8) while opposite side increases.

## Momentum Phases

### Early Momentum Phase

**Characteristics:**
- Light blue waves beginning to form
- Money flow starting to curve (still red for longs)
- RSI building from low levels (weak, needs time)
- DBSI scores transitioning

**Trading Approach:**
- Higher risk, higher reward
- Smaller position sizes
- Wider stops required

### Mid Momentum Phase

**Characteristics:**
- Dark blue waves established
- Money flow thick and directional (green for longs)
- RSI in favorable territory (green, tight, rising)
- DBSI scores strong (14+)
- Trigger wave confirmed

**Trading Approach:**
- Optimal entry zone
- Standard position sizes
- Tighter stops possible
- Highest probability trades

### Late Momentum Phase

**Characteristics:**
- Waves reaching extremes (peak of green momentum)
- Money flow at peak thickness
- RSI approaching overbought/oversold
- Warning symbols appearing (Yellow X, Red X, Blood Diamond)

**Trading Approach:**
- Take profits, don't enter new positions
- Trail stops tightly
- Watch for reversal signals

## Strongest Bullish Signals

### Tier 1 - Highest Probability Long Setups

1. **Deep Anchor + Trigger Wave + Green Dot Combo**
   - Deep anchor wave below zero line
   - Trigger wave forming with light→dark blue transition
   - Smooth, rounded money flow curving up
   - Green dot confirmation
   - Higher timeframe alignment

2. **Multi-Timeframe Green Dot Confluence**
   - Green dot on lower timeframe (4H)
   - Green dot or bullish momentum on higher timeframe (Daily)
   - Money flow green on both timeframes
   - DBSI 14+ bullish score

3. **VWAP + RSI + Money Flow Triple Confluence**
   - VWAP crossing up through zero
   - RSI green, tight, rising
   - Money flow thick green
   - No warning symbols present

## Strongest Bearish Signals

### Tier 1 - Highest Probability Short Setups

1. **Blood Diamond + Yellow X Combo**
   - Blood diamond appears (harshest warning)
   - Yellow X confirms manipulation
   - Money flow turning red
   - Exit all longs immediately

2. **Weekly Engulfing + Inverse JTTW Pattern**
   - Weekly engulfing candle confirmed
   - Inverse "Jesus Take The Wheel" pattern on 4H
   - Multiple timeframe bearish alignment

3. **Head and Shoulders on Momentum**
   - Clear H&S pattern on blue waves
   - Right shoulder lower than left
   - Money flow declining

## Liquidation Points

### Leverage and Liquidation Table

| Leverage | Move Against Position for Liquidation |
|----------|--------------------------------------|
| 2x | 50% |
| 5x | 20% |
| 10x | 10% |
| 20x | 5% |
| 50x | 2% |
| 100x | 1% |

### Identifying Liquidation Zones

**Long Liquidation Zones:**
- Below significant support levels
- Areas where many traders have stop-losses
- Often targeted by whales (Yellow X signals)

**Short Liquidation Zones:**
- Above significant resistance levels
- Areas where short sellers have stop-losses
- Squeeze targets during bullish momentum

### Protection Strategies

1. **Never use more than 10x leverage** for swing trades
2. **Split positions** into 2-4 exits instead of single stop-loss
3. **Use Market Cipher SR** for stop-loss placement
4. **Watch for Yellow X** signals indicating manipulation

## The Blue Wave Strategy

### Entry Checklist

**For Long Entries:**
- [ ] Price ABOVE 200 EMA (DBSI)
- [ ] Deep anchor wave below zero line
- [ ] Trigger wave forming (light→dark blue transition)
- [ ] Blue waves showing higher low with GAP
- [ ] Green dot appearing
- [ ] Money flow smooth and curving up
- [ ] RSI green, tight, rising
- [ ] No warning symbols (Yellow X, Blood Diamond)
- [ ] Higher timeframe confirms bullish bias

**For Short Entries:**
- [ ] Price BELOW 200 EMA (DBSI)
- [ ] Deep anchor wave above zero line
- [ ] Trigger wave forming downward
- [ ] Blue waves showing lower high with GAP
- [ ] Red dot appearing
- [ ] Money flow red and curving down
- [ ] Warning symbols present
- [ ] Higher timeframe confirms bearish bias

### Exit Strategy

**Take Profit Zones:**
- Peak of green momentum wave
- When warning symbols appear
- When money flow reverses
- At Market Cipher SR resistance/support
- 2x risk as standard take profit

**Stop-Loss Placement:**
- At Market Cipher SR levels
- Below anchor wave low (for longs)
- Above anchor wave high (for shorts)
